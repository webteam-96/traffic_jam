About the app

TrafficJam.Life is a Vedic astrology app. It casts your birth chart from the astronomical positions of the planets at the moment you were born, and gives you your Dasha periods, today's Panchang, doshas and remedies. When a chart isn't enough, you can put a question directly to astrologer Jay Kotecha or book a consultation.

Common questions

I don't know my exact birth time. Can I still use the app?

Yes. Your planetary signs, Nakshatra and Dasha periods are all calculated without it. What needs an exact time is the Ascendant, and anything counted from it — the house placements, the KP and Cusp screens, and the D60 chart. The app marks those clearly rather than showing a result it can't stand behind. Add your time later in Profile → Edit Birth Data and everything fills in.

My birth details are wrong. How do I change them?

Profile → Edit Birth Data. Your charts, Dasha timeline and daily readings are all recalculated from the new details straight away.

When will Jay answer my question?

Within the window of the response priority you chose when you asked — four hours on Standard, one hour on Priority. Replies appear in the same conversation inside Ask Jay, and you'll find every question you've asked under Ask Jay → My Questions.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions bought through the App Store are managed by Apple, not by us: open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. On Android, open the Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep access until the current period ends.

Can I get a copy of my data, or delete my account?

Both are in the app, under Profile → Privacy. Export my data gives you your profile, birth data, questions and appointment history. Delete account permanently removes your profile, birth data, charts and consultation history — this cannot be undone.

Is a reading from the app the same as advice?

No. TrafficJam.Life is offered for guidance and reflection. It is not a substitute for professional medical, legal or financial advice, and nothing in the app should be treated as such.

Reporting a problem

If something looks wrong in a chart, please tell us what you expected and what you saw, and include the birth date, time and place involved — an astrological calculation can't be checked without them. Screenshots help.

For anything account-related, please write from the email address on the account or include the mobile number it uses, so we know we're talking to the right person.
